## Deposit insurance (DICGC)
The Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation insures bank deposits up to 5 lakh rupees per depositor per bank, covering savings, current, fixed and recurring deposits together. If you hold more than this amount, consider spreading it across banks.

## Fixed deposits and interest
A fixed deposit pays a fixed rate for a chosen term. Interest is taxable as per your slab, and banks deduct TDS when interest crosses the annual threshold set in the Income Tax Act. Senior citizens usually receive a higher rate. Premature withdrawal usually carries a penalty.

## Preventing UPI and card fraud
Never share your OTP, UPI PIN, card CVV or net-banking password with anyone, including people claiming to be from your bank. Entering your UPI PIN is only needed to send money, never to receive it. Banks and the RBI do not ask for such details by phone or message. Verify links before clicking and avoid screen-sharing apps with unknown callers.

## If you are a victim of fraud
Report the fraud immediately to your bank and call the national cyber-crime helpline 1930, or file a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in. Reporting quickly improves the chances of recovery. Keep screenshots and transaction references.

## Complaints against banks
If a bank does not resolve a complaint within 30 days, you can approach the RBI Integrated Ombudsman Scheme through the RBI complaint portal at no cost.
