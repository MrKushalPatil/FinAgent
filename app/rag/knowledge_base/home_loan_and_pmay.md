## Typical home loan terms
Home loans in India usually run up to 30 years. Lenders commonly finance 75 to 90 percent of the property value depending on the loan size, so the buyer arranges the remaining down payment. Stamp duty, registration and moving costs are extra and are not covered by the loan.

## Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY)
PMAY is a central government housing programme. Earlier PMAY-Urban gave a credit-linked interest subsidy to eligible economically weaker, low-income and middle-income households buying their first home. A newer phase, PMAY-Urban 2.0, was launched in 2024 and offers an interest subsidy on loans for eligible urban households. Eligibility depends on household income, not owning a pucca house and other conditions. Subsidy amounts and rules are revised, so confirm current details on the official PMAY portal before relying on them.

## Income tax deduction on home loan interest
Under the old tax regime, interest paid on a loan for a self-occupied home is deductible under Section 24(b), up to 2 lakh rupees a year. Principal repayment qualifies under Section 80C within the overall 1.5 lakh rupee limit. The new tax regime does not allow the self-occupied home loan interest deduction. Check the current rules for the financial year.

## Home loan affordability rule of thumb
A common guideline is to keep the home EMI, together with other EMIs, within about 40 percent of net monthly income, and to keep an emergency fund of 6 months of expenses after paying the down payment. Test a few tenures and rates with the EMI calculator before choosing.
